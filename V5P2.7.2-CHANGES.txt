MarketLedger Pro V5P.2.7.2 — Per-Symbol Fresh-Candle Recovery Guard

- Preserves V5P.2.7.1 provider entitlement/provenance behavior.
- Candle freshness is derived from the actual final candle timestamp.
- Freshness authority: CURRENT <=15m; AGING 16-45m; STALE 46-60m; EXPIRED >60m.
- Any Yahoo candle older than 45m triggers an independent cache-busted query2 Yahoo recovery request.
- Alpaca IEX is considered only as an additional fallback candidate when configured.
- The freshest valid candidate wins; HTTP 200 alone never establishes freshness.
- Failed recovery never rewrites an old candle to 0m; its true age remains visible and existing setup-promotion guards remain authoritative.
- Response diagnostics expose source, candle age, freshness state and whether recovery was attempted.
- No SIP, delayed_sip, or BOATS historical probes were restored.
- V5N.1 scoring, news/catalyst logic, sector logic, liquidity/risk gates and V5O research logic are unchanged.
