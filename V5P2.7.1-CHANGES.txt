V5P.2.7.1 — Overnight Tape Provenance & Entitlement Guard

- Stops recurring BOATS historical requests after live diagnostics established HTTP 403 entitlement denial.
- Overnight Alpaca endpoint now retrieves only the supported feed=overnight latest bar; no missing history is synthesized.
- Diagnostics no longer probe BOATS, SIP, or delayed_sip. They report usable IEX, overnight-latest, and Yahoo evidence.
- Candle payloads preserve provider + quality metadata down to each browser-side candle.
- Accumulation now separates provider bars, genuine provider-volume bars, latest-only bars, and price-only observations.
- VWAP, estimated directional volume, and volume acceleration use genuine positive-volume provider bars only.
- If fewer than 3 genuine provider-volume bars exist, accumulation confidence is capped; latest-only evidence alone cannot create high-confidence volume intelligence.
- Radar no longer labels all observations simply as EXT/PM bars; it exposes provider-volume/latest-only/price-only counts and suppresses estimated up-volume when evidence is insufficient.
- PRE tape preserves original provider provenance and never converts price-only observations into volume-bearing bars.
- Existing candle freshness authority, entity/news guards, execution thresholds, spread/liquidity gates, and BUY/STRONG thresholds remain unchanged.
