MarketLedger V5P.2.8 — True Overnight Buy/Sell Pressure Intelligence

- Corrects the visible header to V5P.2.8.
- Separates price-structure evidence from real provider-volume evidence.
- Price-only/latest-only observations never manufacture volume.

- STRONG ACCUMULATION requires positive net pressure plus price above extended-session VWAP and confidence gates.
- DISTRIBUTION is explicitly identified when estimated directional volume is materially negative.
- Buy/sell pressure is explicitly candle-direction estimated volume, not exchange aggressor-side order flow.
- V5P.2.7.2 candle freshness/recovery and stale-data blocking remain intact.
