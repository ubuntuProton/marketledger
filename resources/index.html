Time-aware signal legend

STRONG SETUP Strong technical alignment after the current market-phase gate permits entries.

BUY SETUP Positive alignment with confirmation; not shown during opening no-chase periods.

HOLD Trend remains constructive but a fresh entry is extended or confirmation is incomplete.

WAIT & SEE Opening volatility, lunch/afternoon uncertainty, stale data, or conflicting signals.

AVOID / NOT NOW Failed VWAP/trend/volume structure, excessive chase risk, or other technical failure.

Important: These labels are rule-based technical indicators, not guarantees or personalized investment advice. Candles can reverse quickly, data may be delayed, and the engine does not know your portfolio, taxes, risk tolerance, options exposure, breaking news, or order-book liquidity. Use the score as decision support, not an automatic trade instruction.
