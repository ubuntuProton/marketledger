MarketLedger Pro V5M.8.2.1 — Catalyst Freshness Guard

- Preserves V5M.8.2 source-focused catalyst discovery and V5M.8.1 entity-resolution guards.
- Adds publication-age normalization before catalyst classification.
- 0–24 hours: eligible for realtime catalyst classification.
- 24–72 hours: NEW_CATALYST is downgraded to FOLLOW_THROUGH / recent context.
- 3–7 days: BACKGROUND only; cannot become current supporting/actionable evidence.
- Older than 7 days, invalid dates, or materially future-dated timestamps: rejected from realtime catalyst evidence.
- Adds stronger age penalties as defense in depth.
- Applies the same freshness guard to company-specific and crypto-theme news.
- Does not change technical BUY/STRONG thresholds, V5N.1 evidence thresholds, V5P.2.4 session logic, chase/spread/liquidity gates, or watchlist persistence.
