MarketLedger Pro V5N.1 — Pre-Open Evidence Fusion

Adds a decision-support evidence-fusion layer to Continuous Session Intelligence.
It combines the already-classified V5M news/catalyst context with pre-market participation,
volume acceleration, PM VWAP persistence, 30-minute momentum, sector/market confirmation,
quote quality, spread, event risk and gap/chase risk.

Pre-open conclusions: PRE-OPEN SETUP, EARLY LEADER, WATCH AT OPEN, EXTENDED — WAIT, NO EDGE.
Each radar card shows Evidence Fusion score, evidence strength, supporting evidence and risks.

Safety/integrity:
- V5M.7.1 news classification is unchanged.
- Existing BUY/STRONG technical thresholds are unchanged.
- News cannot create BUY/STRONG.
- Evidence Fusion is an attention/decision-support layer, not a probability or prediction.
